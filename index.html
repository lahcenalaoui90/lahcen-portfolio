Amber Mirage Resort

Agafay–Marrakech, Morocco · 2021 · Luxury Eco-Resort
+ Click for details
Architecture

Luxury eco-resort conceived as a dialogue between architecture and desert landscape. Set on the undulating plateaus of Agafay, the project unfolds as terraced volumes merging with the topography — embracing the horizon rather than the skyline.

Material palette of rammed earth, local stone, tadelakt, wood, and brass grounds the project in Moroccan tradition through contemporary geometry. Bioclimatic design: passive cooling, solar energy, water-recycling for irrigation.

Plans and details are confidential.
